# AmsterdamUMCdb External Validation

External validation of the frozen Sepsis Agentic-LLM CDSS execution engine
(`sepsis_agentic_cdss.py`) on the Amsterdam University Medical Centers
Database (AmsterdamUMCdb), conducted after model freeze with no refitting,
recalibration, or threshold adjustment.

## Cohort

- Source: AmsterdamUMCdb (Amsterdam University Medical Centers, 2003-2016),
  23,106 ICU admissions, 20,109 unique patients (first ICU admission).
- Suspected infection (Sepsis-3 / Seymour convention): antibiotic
  administration (`Injecties Antimicrobiele middelen`) paired with a body
  fluid culture order (`procedureorderitems`); if antibiotics precede the
  culture, the culture must follow within 24 h; if the culture precedes
  antibiotics, antibiotics must follow within 72 h.
- Sepsis-3 onset: first ICU hour with 24-h rolling SOFA-2 total >= 2
  (baseline assumed 0), computed with the Ranzani JAMA 2025 SOFA-2
  thresholds (see `build_sofa2_windows.py` header for documented
  footnote-level simplifications).

## Pipeline

| Script | Role |
|---|---|
| `pass1_numeric_scan.py` | Stream-enumerate `numericitems` from the nested deflate64 zip; global item dictionary + broad clinical filter |
| `build_suspected_infection.py` | Antibiotic/culture pairing -> suspected-infection onset per admission |
| `pass3_cohort_numeric.py` | Targeted slim extraction of cohort rows (vitals, blood gas, chemistry, urine output) |
| `pass2_filter_drug_list.py` | Vasopressor/antimicrobial and GCS/ventilation row filters for `drugitems` / `listitems` |
| `build_sofa2_windows.py` | Hourly SOFA-2 subscores (brain/resp/cardiovascular/liver/kidney/hemostasis) + window matrix |
| `run_cdss_validation.py` | Frozen engine application and evaluation |
| `imputation_portability.py` | Site-adapted XGBoost refit (original hyperparameter grid) vs frozen formula and comparators on masked complete cases |

## Evaluation

1. **Agent 2 imputation fidelity** - complete-case masking of measured
   6 h/12 h lactate; frozen reconstruction formula vs linear interpolation
   and mean imputation (MSE, MAE, R2).
2. **Agent 3 alert performance** - frozen audit rules
   (Delta SOFA-2 >= 2, lactate > 2.0 mmol/L with clearance < 10%,
   HR SD < 10 bpm with Delta SOFA-2 > 0) against a deterioration reference
   of 28-day mortality or vasopressor escalation within 48 h
   (accuracy, sensitivity, specificity, Cohen's kappa, AUROC, AUPRC).
3. **Reclassification** - share of indeterminate cases (missing 6 h/12 h
   lactate) reclassified to high-risk (RED) after frozen imputation.

Results are written to `validation_metrics.json` and
`validation_patient_level.csv` (not committed; regenerated by the scripts).

## Data access

AmsterdamUMCdb is available to credentialed researchers via
https://amsterdammedicaldatascience.nl/amsterdamumcdb/ after completion of
the required data-use procedures. No AmsterdamUMCdb patient-level data are
distributed in this repository.

## Engine v2 (interpolation-first hybrid) — `engine_v2/`

Post-hoc architectural revision motivated by the portability findings
(labelled as such in the manuscript; not part of the frozen validation):

- **Arm A (bracketing anchors available):** piecewise-linear interpolation
  between measured lactate anchors. Repeated-CV on all 866 complete cases:
  MSE 1.55, R2 0.72 — the honest information ceiling of the task.
- **Arm B (no later anchor):** anchor-free XGBoost fallback
  (lac0, HR SD, RR SD, age, male, vasopressor escalation; lr 0.01,
  depth 3, subsample 0.6) trained on MIMIC-IV complete trajectories
  (n=1,061), evaluated on AmsterdamUMCdb with 24 h/48 h anchors masked:
  MSE 3.20, R2 0.43 (v1 formula: 3.12 / 0.44).
- **Ceiling diagnostic:** in-site XGBoost R2 0.64; in-sample oracle bound
  0.84; R2 0.9 would require MSE <= 0.44 on this test set and is
  unattainable. Physiological residual correction adds no material gain
  (0.728 vs 0.722).
- **Coverage:** of 2,084 patients missing 6 h or 12 h lactate, only 325
  (15.6%) have a later anchor; 1,759 (84.4%) are information-limited.
  Conclusion: measurement density, not model architecture, is the binding
  constraint in sparse-sampling ICUs.

Files: `ceiling_diagnostic.py`, `ceiling_search.py`, `engine_v2.py`
(cross-site MIMIC-trained variant), `engine_v2_final.py` (final
architecture + fallback training/saving), trained fallback models
(`engine_v2_lac{6,12}_anchorfree.json`, ready for deployment), and
machine-readable results. MIMIC-IV patient-level training data are NOT
redistributed (PhysioNet DUA).
